# Technical Specifications

## General

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Engine** | GameMaker |
| **Language** | GML |
| **Genre** | 2D Space Exploration / Trading / Combat |
| **Art Style** | Pixel Art |
| **Aspect Ratio** | 16:9 (Landscape) |
| **Base Resolution** | **480 × 270** |
| **Target FPS** | 60 FPS |

---

## Target Platforms

- Android
- iOS
- Desktop (Future)

---

## Rendering

| Setting | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Render Resolution | 480 × 270 |
| Pixel Filtering | Disabled |
| Sprite Scale | 1:1 |
| Camera | Orthographic |
| HUD | GUI Layer |
| VSync | Enabled |

---

## Camera

- Camera follows the player.
- The game world is rendered at **480 × 270**.
- The viewport scales to fit the device.
- Wider screens reveal more horizontal space instead of stretching the image.

---

## Sprite Guidelines

### Ships

| Type | Size |
|------|-----:|
| Small Ship | 16 × 16 px |
| Medium Ship | 24 × 24 px |
| Large Ship | 32 × 32 px |

---

### Celestial Bodies

| Type | Size |
|------|-----:|
| Small Planet | 64 × 64 px |
| Large Planet | 128 × 128 px |

---

## Asset Rules

- All sprites must be created at their native resolution.
- Do not upscale sprites before importing.
- Pixel-perfect rendering only.
- Avoid anti-aliasing.
- Prefer dimensions that are multiples of 8 pixels.
